\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Across these cohorts, source-selected center balancing and multiple prototypes provide no consistent advantage over learned linear and cosine heads. UNI and CONCH raise histology point estimates, while learned heads retain higher primary-target BA. Source selection also changes the effective mechanism, choosing the single-centroid limit for CONCH and four prototypes for UNI under the four-candidate protocol. Together with the group-pair and aggregate diagnostics, these findings motivate separating representation quality, selected classifier complexity, and prediction unit. Independent patient-linked data, audited pretraining overlap, full official-split evaluation and modern DG controls remain necessary for broader claims.
